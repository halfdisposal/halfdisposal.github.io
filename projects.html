GDPPO

A MLPack backended gdextension for training and inference of models with support for saving and loading mlpack models. Mainly focused on Reinforcement Learning with PPO Algorithm, with support for Linear layer and activation functions.

AtelierMagic

A spell-casting game where you draw glyphs freehand, inspired by the anime Witch Hat Atelier. Strokes are scored using connected-component analysis and PCA-based normalization, matched against a library of gestures.

  • Godot 4
  • GDScript
  • Computer Vision
