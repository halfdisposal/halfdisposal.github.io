About

I'm a physics student working through a BS-MS degree in IISER Kolkata, currently deep in quantum mechanics, condensed matter physics and exploring phase transitions in materials, alongside the usual run of experimental labs.

Outside coursework, I build software and games under the name Halfdisposal. Most of it sits at the intersection of the two halves of my life: physical simulations that need to be fast and correct, and the engines and editors that make them explorable. I work mainly in C++, Rust, GDScript, and Python.
